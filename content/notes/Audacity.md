Summary:

1) Highlight Audio which you want to make it to sound good 
2) Effect > Noise Reduction (Remove background noise) 
3) Effect > Filter Curve > Manage > Factory Presets > repeat for these three things, and click OK after each thing: 
 a) Bass Boost 
 b) Treble Boost 
 c) Low rolloff for Speech 
4) Effect > Compressor (leave the setitings & just press OK) 
5) Effect > Limiter > Set to Hard Limit > click OK 

Other tips: 
1) it helps A LOT to have the mic close to mouth when recording 
2) Audio tracks in Audacity have 2 parts: top and bottom. If one part is completely flat (aka no sound), make the track MONOTONE. Do this by clicking Audio Track > Split Stereo to Mono > delete the flat part.
